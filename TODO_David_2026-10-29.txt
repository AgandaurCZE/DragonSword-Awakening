1. SKILL LEVELS NOT MEASURED YET (quick, in the codex)
   The site shows these as "(est.)" until measured.
   - One level-10 skill at Lv.6, 7, 8 and 9 (e.g. Reina – Wind Grab).
   - One level-7 skill at Lv.6 (e.g. Johnny – Dirty Fight).
   - Optional: Lv.2–5 of one skill from a different hero, to confirm the
     multipliers are the same for everyone.

2. KARMA ASCENSIONS NOT MEASURED YET
   - Any Karma you can see at A3, A4 and A5. Frozen Fortress or
     A Flame That Cuts Through the Moment are best, since A0–A2 are already
     recorded for them.

3. LEVEL 40 (once you have the breakthrough materials)
   - Any hero except Charlotte: Attack, Defense, Max HP at level 40 BEFORE
     the breakthrough, then AFTER it. Read them on the levelling screen
     (no titles there).
   - Charlotte too if you can, since her breakthroughs differ.
   - The guide predicts 426 / 132 / 2852 before the breakthrough
     (440 / 127 / 2777 for Charlotte).

4. TRAINING DUMMY (Test Kit tests 2–8, in this order)
   - Damage spread: 10–20 non-crit hits of one single-hit skill.
   - Attack scaling: same skill, before and after a gear swap that only
     changes Attack.
   - Crit damage: one normal hit and one crit with the same skill.
   - Crit chance: about 200 hits at around 50% Crit Rate, count the crits.
   - Defense Ignore / Armor Penetration, status bonuses and buff stacking
     if the dummy allows statuses and buffs.
   - First check what the dummy lets you set (level, defense, statuses),
     and note it in the chat.

5. TITLES

6. GEAR SUBSTATS
   - Do green, blue and purple pieces roll the same substat values as
     orange ones? Compare one substat (e.g. Crit Rate on a helmet)
     across rarities.

7. STATUS TICKS
   - If damage numbers appear while Poison, Burn or Bleed tick on an enemy,
     note a few values and how often they appear.
