-------------------------------------------------------------- ONCE YOU HAVE EILEEN

10. EILEEN'S TEAM CRIT BUFF
   - Vanguard Banner: how long the +20% Crit Rate buff lasts, its cooldown or
     Rage cost, and how often you can recast it during a normal team rotation.
   - With her Exclusive Karma (Between Chivalry and Bookmarks): the extra
     Crit Rate and Crit Damage when Banner Maid is used inside the Brave One
     area (the patch notes say Crit Damage 20%; the Crit Rate part is unknown).
   - Brave One: duration of the +15% Attack.
   Why: our builds already sit at about 100% Crit Rate, so her buff does
   nothing for teammates right now. A teammate built for her (e.g. Charlotte
   with Crit Damage gloves instead of Crit Rate) gains about +11% while the
   buff is up and loses about 2% while it's down. Uptime decides whether
   "with Eileen" builds are worth it, and how high Eileen should rank.

     difficulty; a lower plain hit only means more Defense.

Tier list decisions so far (agreed 29 Oct 2026, not on the page yet; they go into TIER_LIST_V2.heroes once tiers are set)
- Kalien: needs Awakening 1. David: close to unplayable without it (Super Armor break is tedious on some enemies, reliable breaking pushes you to bring a Breaker, and she has few teams).
- Reina: no awakening needed as a pure enabler (strong from A0); needs Awakening 3 when played as Main DPS or Sub-DPS.
- Ryza: A1 open. David doesn't own her yet, so he can't judge how clunky she is without it. Airborne only counts while the enemy is in the air, so A1's value depends on how long she (and her team) can keep enemies up. Without A1 her only Airborne is the Ultimate (Stormbringer); A1 adds it to Flash. Decide once David owns her or has seen her played. Likely Expert tag (David, from how Airborne works).
- How Airborne plays (David, 29 Oct, from Reina): the bonus only lasts while the enemy is in the air, and her lifts are short on their own. Wind Slayer (Ultimate dash) barely lifts enemies off the ground; Rising Cutter (Skill) lifts them high, but its downward finisher ends Airborne right away. In the Venom team, quick-swapping and rotating everyone's Specials keeps enemies up much longer, and all those Specials on Airborne enemies raise team damage a lot. So Airborne heroes are much stronger in a team built to keep enemies up than on their own. Use for Reina's and Ryza's tags and texts.
- Castella: no "Needs awakening" tag; gets the Easy tag. David: works fine as she is, even in teams she doesn't fit well, because she breaks enemies fast and broken enemies don't act for the 8 s (big QoL for newer and casual players). Play: spin, break, apply Stun, swap. A1 (Knockdown on Stun Blow) mainly lets her stand in for a dedicated Knockdown enabler in more teams.
