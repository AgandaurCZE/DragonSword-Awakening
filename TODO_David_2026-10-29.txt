top-down, whenever you feel like it. Send results in a chat message (or the

-------------------------------------------------------------- MOST USEFUL

1. STATUS BONUSES – DONE (28 Oct): on hits against an enemy with the status,
   "Additional Attack Power" adds to Attack and "Additional Damage" multiplies.
   They apply to the whole hit, including other statuses' bonuses (410 test).
   Optional: a hero with bonuses for TWO statuses (e.g. Burn Karma + a
   Knockdown set/awakening) – do the two Additional Damage % add or multiply?

     the breakthrough, then AFTER it (levelling screen, no titles there).
   - Charlotte too if you can, since her breakthroughs differ.
   - The guide predicts 426 / 132 / 2852 before the breakthrough
     (440 / 127 / 2777 for Charlotte).
   Why: all level-70 hero stats on the site are estimates from Lv1–30.

3. TITLES

-------------------------------------------------------------- USEFUL

4. SKILL BUFFS BY LEVEL
   - Invisible (Charlotte): the Critical Damage buff at every level,
     Lv.1 to Lv.10 (it's +10% at Lv.1 and +15% at Lv.10).
   - Any other skill whose buff, heal or duration shows a number, if you
     notice one changing with level.
   Why: the level slider only changes damage % at the moment; with these
   it could change buffs correctly too.

5. "ALL STATUS EFFECT ADDITIONAL ATTACK POWER" SUBSTAT
   - Patch 1.1.0 calls it a % increase of every status Additional Attack
     Power; our gear table stores it as a flat 31-45. How does it show on
     your gear: with or without a % sign? A screenshot of one piece is enough.
   - (Crit chance no longer needs testing: 1.1.0 says crits happen exactly
     at your Crit Rate and monsters have no crit resistance.)
   - Optional: do status ticks ever crit? Watch 20 or so ticks.

6. BLUE / PURPLE / GREEN GEAR
   - Blue main stats don't keep one ratio to Orange, and Blue substats are
     half or a quarter of Orange depending on the stat.
   - Most useful: one piece's main stat at every upgrade level (+0 to +9),
     and its substats. Any rarity.

-------------------------------------------------------------- ONCE YOU HAVE EILEEN

10. EILEEN'S TEAM CRIT BUFF
   - Vanguard Banner: how long the +20% Crit Rate buff lasts, its cooldown or
     Rage cost, and how often you can recast it during a normal team rotation.
   - With her Exclusive Karma (Between Chivalry and Bookmarks): the extra
     Crit Rate and Crit Damage when Banner Maid is used inside the Brave One
     area (the patch notes say Crit Damage 20%; the Crit Rate part is unknown).
   - Brave One: duration of the +15% Attack.
   Why: our builds already sit at about 100% Crit Rate, so her buff does
   nothing for teammates right now. A teammate built for her (e.g. Charlotte
   with Crit Damage gloves instead of Crit Rate) gains about +11% while the
   buff is up and loses about 2% while it's down. Uptime decides whether
   "with Eileen" builds are worth it, and how high Eileen should rank.

     difficulty; a lower plain hit only means more Defense.

Tier list decisions so far (agreed 29 Oct 2026, not on the page yet; they go into TIER_LIST_V2.heroes once tiers are set)
- Kalien: needs Awakening 1. David: close to unplayable without it (Super Armor break is tedious on some enemies, reliable breaking pushes you to bring a Breaker, and she has few teams).
- Reina: no awakening needed as a pure enabler (strong from A0); needs Awakening 3 when played as Main DPS or Sub-DPS.
- Ryza: A1 open. David doesn't own her yet, so he can't judge how clunky she is without it. Airborne only counts while the enemy is in the air, so A1's value depends on how long she (and her team) can keep enemies up. Without A1 her only Airborne is the Ultimate (Stormbringer); A1 adds it to Flash. Decide once David owns her or has seen her played. Likely Expert tag (David, from how Airborne works).
- How Airborne plays (David, 29 Oct, from Reina): the bonus only lasts while the enemy is in the air, and her lifts are short on their own. Wind Slayer (Ultimate dash) barely lifts enemies off the ground; Rising Cutter (Skill) lifts them high, but its downward finisher ends Airborne right away. In the Venom team, quick-swapping and rotating everyone's Specials keeps enemies up much longer, and all those Specials on Airborne enemies raise team damage a lot. So Airborne heroes are much stronger in a team built to keep enemies up than on their own. Use for Reina's and Ryza's tags and texts.
- Castella: no "Needs awakening" tag; gets the Easy tag. David: works fine as she is, even in teams she doesn't fit well, because she breaks enemies fast and broken enemies don't act for the 8 s (big QoL for newer and casual players). Play: spin, break, apply Stun, swap. A1 (Knockdown on Stun Blow) mainly lets her stand in for a dedicated Knockdown enabler in more teams.

On hold (David, 29 Oct: not possible with his current roster without spending an awakening on Castella) — possible test (Castella A1 adds Knockdown to Stun Blow): Charlotte's first auto-attack click on the Training Ogre with Stun + Airborne + Knockdown and no Poison/Bleed. x4.0 (88) = bonuses keep adding; x3.5 (77) = a cap, which would also explain the earlier goblin result.

  - Expert tag: AGREED. Reason: you have to think about which skill to use when (Poison/Stealth for Illusion Kick, Airborne for Shadow Grab, Stealth for the Crit Damage), and she picks off the biggest threats one at a time. Drop: -2 (S -> B), AGREED 17:22, REPLACED 17:36 by A with -1 (see TIER AGREED). David: if you can't land Shadow Grab efficiently she does little on her own, and gives the team nothing but the [Clone], which at A0 only draws enemy attention.

The slam only hits the grabbed enemy hard; others nearby take about 42. So: strong single target, weak on groups.
  - Best case: target Knocked down + Airborne + Poisoned with Stealth up = serious damage on one enemy (status bonuses add: Airborne + Knockdown + Poison = x3.5 in the goblin tests, plus Invisible's +15% Crit Damage).
