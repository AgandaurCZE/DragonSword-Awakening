DragonSword Team Planner

Team ideas and the Signature Karma farming order, for David and Claude to fill in together. Everything you change here saves straight away, and Claude can read it.

Connecting…

Claude's suggestions, worked out from each hero's statuses and Activation Conditions, not from the guide's Teams page. None are tested unless the verdict says so. The numbers beside a team name are damage in 1 minute on the Training Ogre, all Lv.70, no gear: normal Lv.1 Karma on the left, signature Karma on the right (green is the better one, the arrow shows which way). Details are in David's notes. One run can be off by up to about 70k, so compare several. Teams built around the same damage dealer share one box: the main version (highest damage with normal Karma) shows first, and the buttons on the right open the alternatives, where one or two heroes are swapped. Each alternative can be edited like any team; × Back returns to the main version. A team doesn't need a Main DPS: two Sub-DPS with an Enabler, or one Sub-DPS with two Enablers, are fine if the synergy is there.
