# DragonSword: Awakening, a fan-made guide

A free guide to **DragonSword: Awakening** (HOUND13, PC / Steam), built from in-game measurements, screenshots and the in-game codex.

**Open the guide:** https://agandaurcze.github.io/DragonSword-Awakening/

What's inside:

- **Beta home:** shortcuts to Database, Dungeons, Guides and Tools.
- **Tier list** of all 20 heroes.
- **Hero pages:** skills, Karmas, gear sets, main stats and team synergy.
- **Teams, Gear and Karma** pages, plus Dungeons, Familiars, Titles, Cookbook, Item Database, Achievements, Damage and Levelling Calculators, and Backup & Restore. Guides are coming soon.
- Every number comes from tests in the game; nothing is guessed. The research behind it is in [`docs/`](docs/README.md).

Fan-made guide. Not affiliated with or endorsed by HOUND13. DragonSword: Awakening and all game assets belong to their respective owners.
