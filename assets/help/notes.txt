STATUS EFFECTS 1/3
Status effects are divided into physical status effects and elemental status effects.
Hitting an enemy with an attack that has a status effect causes that effect to stack, once the stack count reaches a
certain threshold, the target is afflicted with that status effect.
Enemies afflicted with a status effect take increased damage for the duration of the effect.

STATUS EFFECTS 2/3 (Physical Status Effects)
There are 3 types of physical status effects: Stun, Airborne, and Knockdown.
Targets affected by physical status effects have their movement restricted for the duration.

STATUS EFFECTS 3/3 (Elemental Status Effects)
There are 5 types of elemental status effects: Burn, Shock, Frost, Poison, and Bleed, and up to 3 stacks can be applied.
The status effect becomes stronger with each stack.
Targets affected by elemental status effects become more vulnerable to Super Armor-breaking attacks and take elemental
damage corresponding to each element for the duration of the effect.
The Frost status effect reduces the target's overall speed for its duration, and at 3 stacks, the Frozen status is
applied. (last line from David; cut off in the screenshot)

SUPER ARMOR (Super Armor/Break)
All units possess Super Armor.
While Super Armor is active, taking damage will not trigger a hit animation.
When Super Armor is completely destroyed, the unit enters a Break state and becomes vulnerable to physical status effects.
The Break state is lifted after a certain amount of time, and Super Armor is fully restored.
Using Dodge instantly restores all Super Armor.

HOW THIS FITS THE MEASUREMENTS (data.json DAMAGE_TESTS)
- "Increased damage": the hit bonuses (+50% per elemental, +100% per physical status), measured.
- Break itself adds no damage: Charlotte 52 clean -> 118 broken = 52 x 809/359, exactly her Break and All Status
  Additional Attack Power. There is no room for a hidden Defense reduction in that result.
- What Break does instead (game text): opens the enemy to Stun / Airborne / Knockdown. Elemental statuses make the
  enemy easier to break.
- How it plays (David, 31 Oct 2026, from big bosses): physical statuses CAN be applied while Super Armor is up, just
  more slowly, but the enemy keeps moving and attacking. Only after Break does the status actually control it
  (knocked down, launched, stunned in place). So Break turns physical statuses into crowd control; it adds no damage.
