Synthesis:
Previous assumption of needing 10 Runes for it was wrong, Number 10 represents the maximum amouth of Synthesis done at one time.
Needs 5 of the lower grade Runes to turn into a random one tier higher Rune
5 - Damaged Runes = 1 Intact Rune
5 - Intact Runes = 1 Refined Rune
..and so on..
