--- From David's Raid_Runes_Details video and notes (1 Nov 2026, story chapter 5) ---
Runes drop only from the Raid (every tooltip's Main Sources = "Raid").
Five types (name on the card / type line under it), each with a coloured symbol:
  Rune of Determination  (Resolve)       red
  Rune of Protection     (Guardian)      blue
  Rune of Vitality       (Vitality)      green heart   -> Max HP (David's: 308)
  Rune of Focus          (Focus)         gold
  Rune of Amplification  (Amplification) purple        -> Crit Damage (David's: 2.40% and 1.80%)
  Stats of Determination, Protection and Focus: not seen yet. Every tooltip says "Random Option".
Five grades (card colour): Damaged (grey) < Intact (green) < Refined (blue) < Radiant (purple) < Perfect (orange).
Gauron raid rewards seen: Normal (Lv.50) Damaged + Intact runes; Hard (Lv.70) Refined + Radiant runes;
  Very Hard (Lv.90) Perfect runes (the rest of its list wasn't scrolled).
Rules from David: runes can't be upgraded; Synthesis: 5 of one grade -> 1 random of the next (corrected, see below);
  removing an engraved grey (Damaged) rune costs 10,000 Gold.
David owns: Damaged Rune of Amplification x2 (Crit Damage 2.40%, 1.80%), Damaged Rune of Vitality (Max HP 308).

--- Update 1 Nov 2026 (evening): runes_gear_information.txt (David's notes, copied here) ---
Main stats: Determination = Attack, Protection = Defense, Vitality = Max HP, Focus = Critical Rate, Amplification = Critical Damage
  (the same stat on every rune of a type; the value varies within each grade). Values seen are in data.json RUNES.seen.
Synthesis (CORRECTED): 5 runes of the same grade (types can be mixed) -> 1 random rune of the next grade.
  The "10" on the screen is the most syntheses you can queue at once, not a 10-rune requirement.
synthesis-screen.webp: the Synthesis pop-up (5 Damaged runes -> 1 unknown Intact rune).
The same file also has every Disassemble return and upgrade EXP/Gold cost; those are in data.json GEAR_UPGRADE.
