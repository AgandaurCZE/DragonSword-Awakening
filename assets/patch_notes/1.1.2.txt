Hello, this is the DragonSword : Awakening team.

Update 1.1.2 has been applied.
Details of the update are as follows.



■ Improvements

    Changed the key to end measurement in the Training Ground to ESC (keyboard)
        Pressing ESC during DPS measurement now ends the measurement; opening the Main Menu with ESC is available once measurement has ended

    Improved the cutscene that plays during Othello's Hero Quest [The Name Left Behind]



■ Bug Fixes

    Fixed an issue where the Lesser Dragon respawned after being defeated during Adventure Quest [28. The Wanderer of the Wilderness]
    Fixed an issue where defeating the Tower's Specter did not progress properly during Cerese's Hero Quest [Cressila's Upside-Down Tower]
    Fixed an issue where the marker pointed to empty ground instead of the NPC during Othello's Hero Quest [The End of the Man They Sought]
    Fixed an issue where Heavy Attacks triggered more than three times when Othello mounted using Ice Grab
    Fixed an issue where Ryza moved while floating in midair when mounting using Thunder Grab
    Fixed an issue where the explosion damage of clones created by Charlotte's Invisible was intermittently applied twice in a row
    Fixed an issue where Ornette could use Holy Cross twice in a row with her Exclusive Karma equipped
    Fixed an issue where equipment was crafted without consuming the Forging Mold after visiting the Exchange with a Main Option selected during Equipment Crafting
    Quick Slot borders now display correctly on item icons after registering food to a Quick Slot from the Inventory menu
    Fixed an issue where beehives for gathering Honey were not displayed properly
    Fixed an issue where food buffs could be stacked using a specific method
