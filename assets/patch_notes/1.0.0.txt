Hello, this is the DragonSword : Awakening team.

※ This notice applies to players who played the pre-launch build of DragonSword : Awakening.
※ Players starting the game for the first time from today's official launch are NOT affected.

With today's patch, some progression data from the pre-launch build will be reset.
We ask for your understanding regarding the inconvenience of having to rediscover or progress through the affected content again.

The details of the changes are as follows.



■ Cooking Recipes

    Some discovered cooking recipes will be reset and will need to be discovered again.
    Recipes discovered after this patch will be saved normally.



■ Mini-Games

    Some mini-game records will be reset, and you will be able to play them again. However, rewards already claimed cannot be received again.
    Mini-game progress after this patch will be saved normally.



■ Titles / Achievements

    All titles will be reset except for those obtained through Mercenary Corps promotions.
    However, achievement progression values (progress) will be preserved, so you can obtain the corresponding titles again by claiming the achievement rewards.
    Accumulated Fame stage rewards will also be reset and can be claimed again.
    Achievement rewards and titles obtained after this patch will be saved normally.



■ Karma Codex

    Some Karma Codex entries will be reset, and the corresponding Karma will need to be collected again to be recorded in the codex.
    Karma obtained after this patch will be saved normally.



■ Sudden Missions

    Discovery information for Sudden Missions in the Merc Guide will be reset, and locations will need to be rediscovered to be saved again.
    Sudden Mission discovery and progress records after this patch will be saved normally.





Once again, we apologize for the inconvenience.
We will continue to work toward providing a better play experience.

Thank you.

— The DragonSword : Awakening Team
