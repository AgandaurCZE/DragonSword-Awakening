Rift (not released as a mode yet). David's notes, 31 Oct 2026, from the voiced side-quest line unlocked after story
chapter 4 (17 quests). rift-portal.webp: a Rift portal in the open world, with one Corrupted Entity on screen.

STORY
- During the quests the MC (Lute) pulls a child named Ninian out of the Rift; she sees him as her father. At the end she
  evolves like the Corrupted monsters do, but glowing gold, because she was loved. The line ends with her leaving the
  MC asleep in a field of flowers where she evolved. (Title "Doting Parent" came from this line.)

CORRUPTED ENTITIES (official name)
- Never-seen models, black with purple glowing veins. The Rift spawns them.
- On death a Corrupted Entity always drops a black crystal, Crystal of Erosion (official name).
- Other Corrupted Entities can eat these crystals: they turn into an undamageable cocoon and come out as an evolved,
  bigger version, with their HP fully restored.
  Small eats 1 crystal -> Medium. Medium eats 2 -> Large. Large ignores crystals (final stage).
- Resistance rises a lot with each stage (Lv.50 enemies; Reina, uses needed for Airborne):
  Ultimate: Small 1, Medium 2, Large 4. Skill: Small 1, Medium 2, Large 4. All halved when the enemy is Broken.
- Super Armor seems much higher than usual: Lv.90 training goblins break in one hit, these don't.
