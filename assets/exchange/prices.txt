WHAT THE PROG. MATERIALS DO (David, 30 Oct 2026)
- Insignia of <faction>: breakthroughs of that faction's heroes (HEROES[].faction). Item card says Exchange only, but David has many, so likely also Legendary Chests.
- Chronograph of Ages: Karma breakthroughs. Exchange and treasure chests (probably Legendary only).
- Fruit of Vitality, Primordial Seed, Drop of Purity, Leaf of Vigor: hero breakthroughs (card doesn't say which hero/archetype uses which). Gathered in the open world; some Normal Dungeons drop them.
- Crystal of Remembrance / Recollection / Reverie / Oblivion: Karma breakthroughs (card doesn't say which). Mined in the open world; some Normal Dungeons drop them.
- Record of Battle, Secret Text of the Warrior, Secret Text of Victory, Tome of Insight: skill upgrades (below).

TRAIT STONES AND GEAR CRAFTING (David, 30 Oct 2026)
Trait Stones: one per archetype, three grades: Low-Grade (green), Intermediate (blue), High-Grade (purple).
Used for Karma breakthroughs (Karma of that archetype) and for crafting that archetype's gear set.
Transmute: 3 Low-Grade -> 1 Intermediate; 3 Intermediate -> 1 High-Grade (same archetype).

Crafting an archetype set piece (Blue is the lowest craftable rarity):
- Blue:   1 Low-Grade stone + 3 Red Pyroxene + 2 green Crystals
- Purple: 2 Intermediate stones + 3 Tangled Memories and Reminiscences + 3 green Crystals
- Orange: 3 High-Grade stones + 3 Tangled Forgetfulness and Remembrance + 5 Vein Crystals
Green Crystals (open-world mining only): Binding Crystal (green) for Holy Arbiter, Azure Gale, Earthshatterer and Ruinsbreaker
(the physical-status sets); Piercing Crystal (purple) for Venomfog Shade, Blazing Flames, Froststorm, Thunderrush and
Bloodstained Chaser (the damage-over-time sets).
Vein Crystal (blue): probably minable (not found yet); Transmute 3 Binding + 3 Piercing -> 1 Vein Crystal.
The three general sets (The Infiltrator, Ballad of Life, Battle Will) drop in Normal Dungeons.

Where the crafting materials come from:
- Red Pyroxene: Exchange (5 emblems); disassembling Rare (Blue) gear: 3 per +0 piece, 5 per +3 and +6 piece (+1/+2 untested).
- Tangled Memories and Reminiscences: Exchange (10); disassembling Hero-grade (Purple) gear: 3 per piece at any level +0..+12;
  Transmute: 2 Red Pyroxene + 1 Crystal of Remembrance + 1 Crystal of Recollection -> 1.
- Tangled Forgetfulness and Remembrance: Exchange (25); disassembling Orange gear (amount unknown);
  Transmute: 2 Tangled Memories and Reminiscences + 1 Crystal of Reverie + 1 Crystal of Oblivion -> 1.

Karma breakthroughs: Trait Stone amounts known only for Lv20 and Lv30 (to add).

SPECIAL MATERIALS (David, 30 Oct 2026)
- Forging Mold: guarantees the chosen main stat on a crafted piece; 1 per piece at any rarity. Exchange (50); Normal Dungeons (drop rate unknown).
  Every slot can roll every main stat: Attack, Max HP, Defense, Attack Power Up, Max HP Up, Defense Up, Critical Rate, Critical Damage,
  Critical Hit Resistance, Critical Damage Reduction.
- Shape Grinder: reforges (re-rolls) a piece's substats; 1 per reforge for Blue and Purple. Lock substats you want to keep
  (Purple up to 3, Blue up to 2); only the Gold cost rises: 0 locked 5,000 / 1 locked 10,000 / 2 locked 25,000 / 3 locked 50,000.
  Exchange (25), Normal Dungeons (probably rare), Raid.
- Anonymous Artisan's Crafting Tool: 5 unlock one Rune slot. Exchange (100), Raid.
- Moon Key: opens special locked chests in the open world (usually Legendary). Exchange only (20).
