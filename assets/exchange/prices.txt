WHAT THE PROG. MATERIALS DO (David, 30 Oct 2026)
- Insignia of <faction>: breakthroughs of that faction's heroes (HEROES[].faction). Item card says Exchange only, but David has many, so likely also Legendary Chests.
- Chronograph of Ages: Karma breakthroughs. Exchange and treasure chests (probably Legendary only).
- Fruit of Vitality, Primordial Seed, Drop of Purity, Leaf of Vigor: hero breakthroughs (card doesn't say which hero/archetype uses which). Gathered in the open world; some Normal Dungeons drop them.
- Crystal of Remembrance / Recollection / Reverie / Oblivion: Karma breakthroughs (card doesn't say which). Mined in the open world; some Normal Dungeons drop them.
- Record of Battle, Secret Text of the Warrior, Secret Text of Victory, Tome of Insight: skill upgrades (below).

SKILL UPGRADE COSTS (same for every hero and every skill)
Lv2: 1 Record of Battle + 2,500 Gold
Lv3: 2 Record of Battle + 5,000 Gold
Lv4: 3 Record of Battle + 1 Hunt boss material + 10,000 Gold
Lv5: 1 Secret Text of the Warrior + 1 Hunt boss material + 15,000 Gold
Lv6: 2 Secret Text of the Warrior + 1 Hunt boss material + 25,000 Gold
Lv7: 3 Secret Text of the Warrior + 1 Hunt boss material + 35,000 Gold
Lv8: 1 Secret Text of Victory + 1 Hunt boss material + 50,000 Gold
Lv9: 2 Secret Text of Victory + 1 Hunt boss material + 65,000 Gold
Lv10: 3 Secret Text of Victory + 3 Tome of Insight + 90,000 Gold
Skills that max at Lv7 (Auto, Jump, Dash attack): same path, but Lv7 = 3 Secret Text of the Warrior + 1 Tome of Insight + 35,000 Gold (no boss material).
Hunt boss materials follow the Hunt screen order: Spiny Fin (Furcas), Blood-stained Hook (Vanak), ... (which level uses which: to confirm).
