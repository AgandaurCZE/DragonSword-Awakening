Mercenary Crew Level (free reward track, levels 1-80). David's video, 1 Nov 2026; 21 screenshots here (level-track-NN.webp).

- Every level gives one item; every 5th level is a bigger milestone (framed on the right).
- From level 26 on the items rotate between Arcana of Peace (4,000 Karma EXP), Core Combat Text (6,000 Hero EXP)
  and Mana Ore (1,500 Equipment EXP); the amount rises from 5 to about 20 by level 80.
- A second row adds 50 or 100 of two crystal currencies on some levels (icons only; not identified yet).
- Levels 1-25 are already claimed in David's game (ticks cover the amounts).
