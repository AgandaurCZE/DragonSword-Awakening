Raid (from David's screenshots and notes, 1 Nov 2026, story chapter 5)

Introduction (in-game): "Raid is co-op content that up to 3 players can challenge together."
"As raid rewards, you can obtain various equipment, Runes, and special equipment-related materials."
Modes: Multiplayer Mode (Invite Code) and Solo Mode.

Raid 1: Gauron, stage "Steelheart Fortress Defense". Members 1-3.
  Normal     recommended Lv.50  rewards: Damaged + Intact Runes (all 5 types), EXP 800, Gold 150,000, materials (1, 1-3, 30)
  Hard       recommended Lv.70  rewards: Ballad of Life and Infiltrator gear (Orange), Refined + Radiant Runes
  Very Hard  recommended Lv.90  rewards: Perfect Runes, EXP, Gold, materials
  Battle Will gear is also in the reward lists (Raid_Gauron_Rewards_*.webp).
Structure (David): an Ogre boss on the bridge, then Gauron phase 1 (humanoid), then Gauron phase 2 (dragon).
  HP carries over between the fights, so healing food matters.
Raid 2: locked; unlocks after story chapter 8.
