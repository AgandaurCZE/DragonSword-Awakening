WHAT IT IS (in-game help)
- "Challenge content based on designated traits. Form your team to match the Status Effect each tower requires."
- "Clearing a trial lets you earn up to 3 stars per floor, and you can receive additional rewards based on the
  cumulative number of stars earned in each tower."

TOWERS SEEN SO FAR
- Tower of Amplitude, Tower of Gale, Tower of Crushing (entrance doors coloured like the archetypes).
- Tower of Gale text: "He who moves first shall control the flow. He who is as light and swift as the wind
  shall survive and ascend."
- The other archetypes' towers aren't open (yet).

FLOORS (Tower of Gale)
- 30 floors shown. Cumulative rewards go up to 90 stars = 30 floors x 3, so probably no more floors.
- 3 stars per floor, 1 star per condition. The same three kinds of condition on every floor:
  1. Defeat all monsters (boss floors: Defeat the Boss Monster)
  2. Clear within X seconds (shorter limit)
  3. Clear within Y seconds (longer limit)
  The time limits grow with the floor.
  Floor 1: Defeat all monsters / 60 s / 90 s.
  Floor 30: Defeat the Boss Monster / limits blurred in the screenshot (they look like 300 s and 600 s; not confirmed).
- Each floor's first clear gives a fixed number of Fragment of Memory (David, 31 Oct 2026):
  Floors  1-10: 20 25 30 35 40 45 45 50 50 55
  Floors 11-20: 55 60 60 70 70 75 75 80 80 85
  Floors 21-30: 85 90 90 95 95 100 110 120 130 150
  Total for all 30 floors: 2170.

ENTRY AND ENEMIES (David, 31 Oct 2026, Tower of Gale floors 1-5)
- Any hero can enter. The deploy screen only shows "Recommendations" with the tower's archetype icon
  (source/deploy-recommendations.webp).
- Other statuses work: Johnny's Poison applied normally. No immunity bubble, and nothing broke anyone.
- Named (elite) enemies and the floor-5 boss weren't immune to Poison. Poison was harder to build up on the boss,
  but that may be normal boss resistance (David hadn't fought that boss before).
- Enemy level rises by 5 every 2 floors: floors 1-2 Lv.40, floors 3-4 Lv.45, floor 5 Lv.50. Only floors 1-5 seen.
- David's impression: the tower's own archetype gets a boost, probably the same doubled hit bonus as a Trait Dungeon
  weakness (Reina hits very hard there). NOT measured yet. Test: Reina's same hit on the same tower enemy, clean and
  Airborne. x2 = normal; x3 = Airborne's +100% doubled, like a weakness. Johnny's Poison as a check: x1.5 = no boost.

FIRST DAMAGE TEST (David, 31 Oct 2026, Tower of Gale; Reina 626 Attack, Armor Penetration 15, Defense Ignore 0.97%,
Airborne AD 15%, Airborne AAP 250; no crits)
- Clean 2904 | Airborne 41843 | Poison 8712 | Airborne + Poison 62460.
- Outside the tower these would be about x3.2 (Airborne), x1.5 (Poison) and x4.0 (both) of the clean hit.
  Here: x14.4, x3.0 and x21.5. So the tower raises status hits a lot, and not only for Airborne.
- All four on the same enemy type (floor 1 archers; the harpies were killed first), all with Reina's Ultimate.
- Poison alone x3.0 fits Poison's hit bonus being 4x its normal size (+50% -> +200%).
- Airborne doesn't fit a simple rule. If the bonuses still add, the two Airborne numbers give: Airborne bonus about
  +300% (x4.06 with Poison's +200% added on top) and an extra x3.55 on Airborne hits, of which the normal Airborne
  AAP and Additional Damage explain only x1.61. Unexplained; needs a hit without Reina's Airborne gear stats.
