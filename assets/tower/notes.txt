WHAT IT IS (in-game help)
- "Challenge content based on designated traits. Form your team to match the Status Effect each tower requires."
- "Clearing a trial lets you earn up to 3 stars per floor, and you can receive additional rewards based on the
  cumulative number of stars earned in each tower."

TOWERS SEEN SO FAR
- Tower of Amplitude, Tower of Gale, Tower of Crushing (entrance doors coloured like the archetypes).
- Tower of Gale text: "He who moves first shall control the flow. He who is as light and swift as the wind
  shall survive and ascend."
- The other archetypes' towers aren't open (yet).

FLOORS (Tower of Gale)
- 30 floors shown. Cumulative rewards go up to 90 stars = 30 floors x 3, so probably no more floors.
- 3 stars per floor, 1 star per condition. The same three kinds of condition on every floor:
  1. Defeat all monsters (boss floors: Defeat the Boss Monster)
  2. Clear within X seconds (shorter limit)
  3. Clear within Y seconds (longer limit)
  The time limits grow with the floor.
  Floor 1: Defeat all monsters / 60 s / 90 s.
  Floor 30: Defeat the Boss Monster / limits blurred in the screenshot (they look like 300 s and 600 s; not confirmed).
- Each floor's first clear gives a fixed number of Fragment of Memory (David, 31 Oct 2026):
  Floors  1-10: 20 25 30 35 40 45 45 50 50 55
  Floors 11-20: 55 60 60 70 70 75 75 80 80 85
  Floors 21-30: 85 90 90 95 95 100 110 120 130 150
  Total for all 30 floors: 2170.
